THE 20 ENGINEERED FEATURES FOR VIRALITY PREDICTION

This system predicts virality using exactly 20 features extracted from three
sources: the video file itself (visual and audio) and content metadata.
All features describe the content: none describe the creator's audience size.

VISUAL FEATURES: 7 features from video frames

1. BRISQUE Quality Score
Blind/Referenceless Image Spatial Quality Evaluator. Measures perceptual
sharpness and clarity of each frame. Sampled every 10th frame, averaged.
Lower BRISQUE = higher visual quality. Poor quality reduces virality.

2. Color Vibrancy
Mean saturation of the S channel in HSV colour space across sampled frames.
Higher saturation = more vivid, eye-catching visuals. Viral content tends
to be visually bold.

3. Motion Intensity
Optical flow magnitude between consecutive grayscale frames using
Lucas-Kanade method. Measures how much physical movement occurs. High
motion signals energy and dynamism.

4. Face Presence Ratio
Fraction of sampled frames containing at least one detected human face,
using MediaPipe FaceDetection. Human faces drive attention and engagement.

5. Face Emotion: Joy
Average "happy" probability from DeepFace emotion analysis across
face-detected frames. Joy is consistently the highest-engagement emotion
on social media.

6. Face Emotion: Surprise
Average "surprise" probability from DeepFace. Surprise is a strong
attention-capturing emotion associated with viral content.

7. Thumbnail Brightness
Mean pixel intensity of the first frame (the de facto thumbnail).
Bright thumbnails perform better in feed environments where content
competes for visual attention.

AUDIO FEATURES: 5 features from audio track

8. Tempo BPM
Beat rate extracted via librosa.beat.tempo(). Fast-paced audio creates
energy and keeps viewers engaged.

9. RMS Energy
Root Mean Square energy: measures loudness and punchiness. Higher RMS
signals a more impactful, professional audio mix.

10. Speech Ratio
Proportion of audio frames classified as voiced speech using WebRTC VAD
in 30ms windows. High speech ratio indicates direct creator communication.

11. Zero Crossing Rate
Rate at which the audio signal crosses zero amplitude. Distinguishes
noisy/percussive audio (high ZCR) from smooth/tonal audio (low ZCR).

12. Beat Strength
Mean onset strength from librosa.onset.onset_strength(). Measures how
hard and defined each beat hits: stronger beats drive emotional response.

METADATA FEATURES: 8 features from content description

13. Title Sentiment Score
VADER compound sentiment of the title, range -1 to +1. Emotional titles
outperform neutral ones.

14. Title Length
Word count of the title. Optimal range differs by platform.

15. Title Has Question Mark
Binary. Question-format titles invite curiosity and click-through.

16. Title Has Number
Binary. Numbered titles (e.g., "5 Ways to...") signal structured,
digestible content.

17. Description Length
Word count of the description. Longer descriptions signal professionalism
and help with discoverability.

18. Tag Count
Number of tags/hashtags. Tag strategy affects algorithmic reach.

19. Upload Hour
Hour of day (0-23) when posted. Platform algorithms favour content
posted during peak active hours.

20. Upload Day of Week
Day (0=Monday, 6=Sunday). Weekend vs weekday performance varies by category.

ENGAGEMENT RATIOS: included in the 20, computed from collected data

Like-to-View Ratio and Comment-to-View Ratio are derived from YouTube
data at training time. At inference, if no engagement data is available,
these are set to the dataset category mean. LightGBM handles missing
values gracefully.
