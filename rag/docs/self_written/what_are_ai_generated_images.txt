WHAT ARE AI-GENERATED IMAGES

DEFINITION

An AI-generated image is a visual created entirely by an artificial
intelligence model from a text prompt, sketch, or reference image.
No camera was used. No real scene was photographed. The pixels were
synthesised mathematically by a neural network.

TWO GENERATIONS OF GENERATORS

The first major wave of AI image generation used Generative Adversarial
Networks (GANs). A GAN consists of two competing networks: a generator
that creates images and a discriminator that tries to detect fakes. They
train against each other until the generator produces images the
discriminator cannot distinguish from real ones. GANs introduced
characteristic artifacts: checkerboard patterns from transposed
convolutions, unnatural eye symmetry, hair blending errors and
high-frequency noise in uniform regions. Early deepfake detection
methods were largely trained on GAN artifacts.

The second and now dominant wave uses diffusion models. Diffusion models
work by learning to reverse a noise-addition process. During training,
real images have random Gaussian noise progressively added until the
image becomes pure noise. The model learns to denoise: to predict and
remove noise step by step. At inference time, the model starts from pure
noise and iteratively refines it into a coherent image guided by a text
prompt. Examples include Stable Diffusion, DALL-E 3, Midjourney and
Adobe Firefly.

WHY DIFFUSION MODELS LEAVE FORENSIC TRACES

Diffusion models produce characteristic frequency-domain signatures
that differ from real camera images. Real cameras introduce optical
lens blur, sensor noise (shot noise, read noise) and JPEG compression
artifacts in predictable physical patterns. Diffusion models introduce
none of these: instead they produce periodic spectral patterns from
the denoising schedule, characteristic textures from the U-Net
architecture's upsampling operations and uniform noise residuals
across the entire image that do not match any physical camera model.

The Discrete Cosine Transform (DCT) and Spatial Rich Model (SRM) filters
used in this system's Stream 2 are specifically designed to reveal these
frequency-domain differences. Real images cluster in one region of
frequency space. AI-generated images cluster in another. This separation
is highly consistent across different diffusion model architectures.

CIFAKE AND GENIMAGE DATASETS

This system's AI detection capability was trained using CIFAKE (60,000
real images + 60,000 Stable Diffusion v1.4 generated images) and a
subset of GenImage (images from 8 different generators including
Midjourney, Stable Diffusion v1.4/v1.5, ADM, GLIDE, Wukong, VQDM,
and BigGAN). Training across multiple generators prevents the model
from learning one generator's specific artifacts and instead forces it
to learn generator-agnostic forensic signals.
