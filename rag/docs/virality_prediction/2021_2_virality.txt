Paper Title: Multimodal Learning of Social Image Representation by Exploiting Social Relations
Authors: Feiran Huang, Xiaoming Zhang, Jie Xu, Zhonghua Zhao, and Zhoujun Li
Year: 2021

Problem Addressed:
Existing models often overlook the social relations among images (e.g., shared albums or groups), leading to suboptimal embeddings when only modeling content information (visual/text).
Methodology:
They introduced the Correlational Multimodal Variational Autoencoder via Triplet Network (CMVAE-TN). It uses a VAE to learn unified representations of visual and textual content (capturing both common and private information) and a triplet network to embed social links between images.
Key Findings:
The CMVAE-TN consistently outperformed competitive baselines in classification and retrieval. Incorporating link information (social relations) boosted mean average precision by 2%–5%.
Limitations:
While effective, the model primarily focuses on static social links rather than the dynamic, real-time behavioral features required for live "virality" predictions.
